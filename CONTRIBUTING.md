# Contributing

The documentation standards for research notes are part of the review and contribution sections of the
[mps-api-research agent skill](skills/mps-api-research/SKILL.md). They are kept there rather than duplicated in this
file.

By submitting a contribution, you agree to license it according to its location in the repository:

- Contributions under `docs/` are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal, as described in `docs/LICENSE`.
- Contributions anywhere else are licensed under the MIT License, as described in the repository's root `LICENSE`.

A contribution that changes files in both areas is governed by the applicable terms for each file. You must have the
right to submit the contribution under those terms. Identify the origin and license when a contribution incorporates
substantial third-party text, code, images, or other material. Source references and short excerpts used as technical
evidence do not require a separate license notice in every research note.
